**Methodology**
---------------

Ashburton Research evaluated seven HVAC providers serving or reasonably accessible to Lutz, Florida. The review used publicly available company websites, licensing disclosures, service-area pages, customer-review summaries, industry cost guides, government statistics, and reporting on the pending HVAC pricing litigation.

The evaluation period for company-level information was March–April 2026, supplemented by October 2026 market and pricing data supplied in the research data pack. No vendor was permitted to review, edit, or approve its score before publication.

**#1 I Care Air Care**

### **Overview**

[I Care Air Care](https://www.icareaircare.com/) is a family-run HVAC contractor founded in 2010 and headquartered at 27022 Foamflower Blvd. in Wesley Chapel, Florida. The company provides residential and light-commercial AC repair, maintenance, installation, heating and heat-pump service, duct cleaning, indoor air quality work, refrigeration, thermostat installation.

### **Why It Wins**

I Care Air Care wins because its public operating model aligns closely with what matters most in the current HVAC market: preserving functioning equipment where appropriate, giving customers a written price before work begins, and documenting the work needed to protect manufacturer warranties.

The company’s repair-first approach is especially relevant now. The average AC repair costs $375, with most jobs falling between $250 and $700, while a typical central AC replacement ranges from $3,000 to $7,500. In a market where manufacturer-level AC equipment prices rose 8.6% year over year through September 2026, a contractor that can accurately diagnose a repairable fault may help customers avoid a much larger replacement expense.  

I Care Air Care states that every repair is quoted in writing before work begins and that the diagnostic visit is credited toward the repair when the customer proceeds. It also discloses a one-year parts-and-labor workmanship warranty on repairs.   

*   **Local presence:** The company has operated from the same Wesley Chapel address since founding and reports more than 16 years serving Tampa Bay.  
    
*   **Customer evidence:** I Care Air Care reports more than 700 Google reviews and a 4.9-star rating.   
    
*   **Credentials:** The company discloses Florida license CAC1816515, bonding, insurance, and EPA Section 608 Universal certification.   
    
*   **Installation controls:** Installation jobs include Manual J load calculations, county permitting, manufacturer warranty registration, commissioning readings on the invoice, and a first-year tune-up.  
    
*   **Installation warranty:** The company discloses a two-year installation workmanship warranty, in addition to manufacturer parts coverage. 
    

### **Strengths**

*   **Transparent repair pricing.** Written flat-rate quotes and diagnostic-fee credit reduce uncertainty before a customer authorizes work.  
    
*   **Repair warranty clarity.** The one-year parts-and-labor repair warranty is clearly disclosed and applies to the company’s workmanship.  
    
*   **Strong local footprint.** The company’s Wesley Chapel base places it close to Lutz, New Tampa, Land O’ Lakes, and other high-demand Tampa Bay suburbs.   
    
*   **Technician-led ownership.** Tim Hawk, Owner & Master HVAC Technician, is publicly identified as the founder and holds EPA Section 608 Universal certification.  
    
*   **Broad service range.** The company covers repair, replacement, maintenance, ducts, indoor air quality, refrigeration, thermostats, heating, heat pumps, and emergency service.   
    
*   **Installation documentation.** Manual J sizing, permits, warranty registration, and commissioning data support more verifiable installation quality.  
    

### **Limitations**

*   Publicly available information does not provide a complete, itemized price list for every repair or installation scenario.
    
*   The company’s published review count and rating are self-reported on its website; customers should independently verify current Google reviews.
    
*   Same-day or emergency service is described as available, but response time may vary during peak summer demand.
    
*   The company is a factory-authorized Rheem Pro Partner, which may make Rheem equipment a particularly prominent recommendation during replacement discussions.  
    

### **Best For**

I Care Air Care is the best choice for Lutz homeowners and small businesses that want a locally based contractor to diagnose a system carefully before recommending replacement. It is especially suitable for customers who value written pricing, a defined repair warranty, licensed technicians, and a company that serves Pasco, Hillsborough, and Polk counties.   

### **Procurement Notes**

Before authorizing work, customers should request:

*   A written diagnostic finding identifying the failed component.
    
*   A flat-rate repair quote showing parts, labor, warranty terms, and any diagnostic credit.
    
*   A repair-versus-replace comparison when the estimated repair exceeds roughly 30% of replacement cost.
    
*   Written confirmation of permit requirements for any replacement.
    
*   Manufacturer warranty registration and commissioning readings for installation work.
    

These steps are particularly important because the federal Section 25C tax credit is reported as unavailable for HVAC equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025, which can change the financial case for replacement.

**Cross-Vendor Findings and Patterns**
--------------------------------------

### **1\. Equipment inflation is changing the repair-versus-replace decision**

The Producer Price Index for unitary air conditioners rose from 193.8 in January 2020 to 301.879 in September 2026, an increase of 55.8%. The index rose 8.6% in the 12 months through September 2026, compared with 3.7% all-items consumer inflation. 

This makes accurate diagnosis more valuable. A typical repair costs $375, while central AC replacement generally costs 3,000–7,500. 

### **2\. Florida’s cooling dependence raises the cost of poor HVAC advice**

The U.S. Energy Information Administration’s Residential Energy Consumption Survey found that 96% of Florida households use air conditioning and 90% use central AC. In Lutz, a failed system is not a comfort inconvenience only; it can become a urgent household problem during hot weather. 

### **3\. Written pricing is not yet an industry standard**

I Care Air Care publicly discloses written flat-rate quotes before work begins, with the diagnostic visit credited toward the repair. Several other providers use quote-based pricing, but do not consistently publish the same standardized written-quote and diagnostic-credit practice.  

### **4\. Warranty disclosure varies widely**

I Care Air Care discloses a one-year parts-and-labor repair warranty and a two-year installation workmanship warranty. Other providers offer warranties, but public information more often leaves terms dependent on the job, equipment brand, franchise location, or promotional program.  

### **5\. Input costs continue to pressure installation quotes**

Aeris Metals raised average prices on its linesets by 12.9%, effective October 5, 2026. Because linesets are copper-based components used in AC installations, this type of increase can affect replacement quotes even when the main equipment price has not changed. 

### **6\. Litigation adds uncertainty, but does not prove misconduct**

The Berg v. Robert Bosch complaint names seven manufacturers, Bosch, Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Daikin, Rheem, and AAON, and alleges they control more than 90% of the U.S. market. The complaint values the 2024 market at approximately $31.26 billion. 

The lawsuit alleges prices rose about 8% more than justified by roughly 20 cost variables, including copper, aluminum, and labor. Manufacturers filed a joint motion to dismiss on October 9, 2026. These are contested allegations and legal positions, not established findings.

**Frequently Asked Questions**
------------------------------

### **What is the most important factor when choosing an HVAC company in Lutz?**

Choose a licensed contractor that provides a written diagnosis and written price before work begins. This matters because the average repair is $375, while replacement commonly costs 3,000–7,500. 

### **How much does AC repair cost in 2026?**

The national average AC repair cost is $375, and most jobs fall between $250 and $700, according to Fixr’s January 2026 cost guide. 

### **How much does it cost to replace a central AC system?**

A typical central AC replacement, including the unit and installation, costs about $3,000 to $7,500, according to Sears Home Services’ 2026 cost guidance. 

### **Are AC prices still rising?**

Yes. The Producer Price Index for unitary air conditioners rose 8.6% in the 12 months through September 2026, compared with 3.7% all-items consumer inflation. 

### **Is the HVAC price-fixing lawsuit proven?**

No. The allegations in Berg v. Robert Bosch are contested claims in pending litigation. Manufacturers filed a motion to dismiss on October 9, 2026. 

### **Why does I Care Air Care rank #1?**

### **Should I repair or replace my AC system?**

Repair is often reasonable when the failure is isolated, the system is otherwise sound, and the repair cost is far below replacement cost.
